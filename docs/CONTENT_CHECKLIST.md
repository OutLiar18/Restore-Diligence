# Content Confirmation Checklist

## Business

- [ ] Confirm exact public business name and spelling.
- [ ] Confirm whether the descriptor is “Homemade Samoosas & Savouries”.
- [ ] Confirm owner’s public name.
- [ ] Confirm WhatsApp number.
- [ ] Confirm email address.
- [ ] Confirm suburb, collection point and delivery radius.
- [ ] Confirm operating and response hours.
- [ ] Confirm required order notice.
- [ ] Confirm that 24–48 hours after cleared payment is accurate for normal orders.
- [ ] Confirm how additional time for large orders will be communicated.

## Menu

- [ ] Confirm every flavour.
- [ ] Confirm prices and whether pricing is per item, half-dozen or dozen.
- [ ] Confirm minimum order.
- [ ] Confirm fresh/frozen/ready-to-eat options.
- [ ] Confirm spice levels.
- [ ] Confirm vegetarian handling.
- [ ] Add allergen and cross-contamination wording.
- [ ] Confirm platter and bulk-order options.

## Payment and Checkout

- [ ] Confirm official bank name.
- [ ] Confirm account holder exactly as registered.
- [ ] Confirm account number.
- [ ] Confirm account type.
- [ ] Confirm branch code.
- [ ] Confirm where proof of payment must be sent.
- [ ] Confirm whether delivery fees are added before payment.
- [ ] Test the generated order reference and WhatsApp message.
- [ ] Never request card PINs, online-banking passwords or login details.

## Christmas Giving Drive

- [ ] Select and verify the beneficiary organisation/community.
- [ ] Obtain permission to name any partner publicly.
- [ ] Confirm accepted and prohibited goods.
- [ ] Keep the no-money-donations rule visible and unchanged.
- [ ] Confirm collection deadline.
- [ ] Confirm distribution date.
- [ ] Confirm secure drop-off point.
- [ ] Confirm whether collection is available.
- [ ] Decide who records and safeguards donor details.
- [ ] Plan a transparent outcome report.
- [ ] Protect recipient dignity in all photographs and posts.

- [ ] Confirm volunteers will not accept cash, EFT or monetary sponsorships under the drive.
