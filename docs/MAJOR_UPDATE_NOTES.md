# Major Red, Gold, Cart and Giving-Drive Update

## Store identity

- Primary brand colours changed to deep red, burgundy, gold and warm cream.
- Added a refined R–Latin-cross–D monogram in red, burgundy and gold.
- Added the approved horizontal logo lockup with the stacked script wordmark.
- Added responsive WebP and PNG logo assets in `public/brand/`.
- Added browser-tab favicon, 16 px and 32 px icons, Apple touch icon and web manifest.
- Added subtle faith-led language around gratitude, honesty, diligence and service.

## Ordering system

- Products can now be added repeatedly to a persistent browser cart.
- Customers can change quantities, remove products and see an estimated subtotal.
- The cart remains available while navigating or refreshing the website.
- Checkout collects customer, fulfilment, preferred-date and order-note details.
- Checkout creates a unique payment reference and a complete WhatsApp order summary.
- EFT bank details are displayed only as placeholders until the real account is confirmed.
- The website never asks for card numbers, PINs, passwords or banking login details.
- Production wording states that orders are processed within 24–48 hours after payment clears, depending on order size.

## Christmas Giving Drive

- The entire giving-drive route uses a separate green-and-gold identity.
- The mobile action bar changes to giving-drive actions on that page.
- Multiple clear notices state that no cash, EFT or monetary donations are accepted.
- The form only coordinates approved goods such as clothing, food-hamper items, hygiene products, blankets, toys and books.

## Required before launch

Update `src/config/site.js` with the real WhatsApp, email, service area and EFT bank details. Confirm every menu price, order lead time, beneficiary, collection date and drop-off point.
